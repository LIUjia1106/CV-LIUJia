In metals and semiconductors under strong or ultrafast excitation, electrons and phonons can be far from equilibrium with each other: the electrons heat up first and pass their energy to the lattice through electron–phonon coupling. Describing this requires two Boltzmann equations coupled through energy exchange, which adds to the computational cost.

**What I did**

- Developed a general synthetic iterative scheme for the coupled electron–phonon Boltzmann equations, so both carriers converge quickly across transport regimes.

**Papers**

- J. Liu, X. Xiang, Y. Zhou, W. Su. [General synthetic iterative scheme for fast solving coupled electron–phonon Boltzmann equations](/publication/2026-ijhmt-electron-phonon-gsis/). *Int. J. Heat Mass Transfer* 260, 128510 (2026).
